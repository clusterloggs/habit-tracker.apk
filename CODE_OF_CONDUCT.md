# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Habit Tracker project will be treated with respect and dignity, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

**Please:**
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members
- Provide constructive feedback and support

**Examples of positive behavior:**
- Using inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other members

## Unacceptable Behavior

**Please do not:**
- Use sexualized language or imagery
- Make personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Engage in harassment or bullying
- Publish private information without consent
- Make unwanted advances or contact
- Post discriminatory, hateful, or violent content
- Trolling or intentionally disrupting discussions

## Consequences

Community members who violate this code of conduct may be:
- Warned by maintainers
- Temporarily suspended from participation
- Permanently banned from the project

## Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience a violation:
1. **Document** the incident with details
2. **Report** privately to project maintainers
3. **Do not** respond publicly to the violation
